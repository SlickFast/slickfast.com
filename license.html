Questions

What counts as "one app"?
One product your company ships or operates. All internal tools for your own team together count as one app.
Why does commercial use need a license at all?
The engine is AGPL-3.0. Embedding AGPL code in a closed product would require open-sourcing your product too. A commercial license releases you from that obligation — that's the standard dual-license model used by many open-source engines.
Commercial vs. OEM — which am I?
Where does the engine run? On your servers (inside your SaaS or backend) — Commercial. Shipped out the door and installed at your customers' sites — OEM.
Does using the hosted API put AGPL obligations on me?
No. Calling api.slickfast.com is using a service — no code license involved, no copyleft. See the Terms.
Can I evaluate before buying?
Yes — evaluation is free, full-featured, and untimed. Buy when you ship.
