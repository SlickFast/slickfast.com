Alerts

Alerts

Your existing alerts

Nothing to do. Once your Grafana sends its alerts through SlickFast (done at install), every alert you already have arrives as a card: the value, the threshold and the graph of what fired, with Open board and Silence links.

Add a new alert

In your Grafana:

  1. Alerting → Alert rules → + New alert rule.
  2. Name it plainly. It becomes the card's title, e.g. "Checkout errors high".
  3. Query: pick the data source and the metric, the same way you build a dashboard panel.
  4. Condition: e.g. "is above 80". That number becomes the grey line on the card's graph.
  5. Folder and evaluation group (e.g. "Ops", every 1 minute). Pending period: how long it must stay bad before alerting (e.g. 5 minutes, so short blips don't page you).
  6. Optional, for a nicer card: a summary (one sentence), and annotations unit = % (or ms, …) and slickfast_board = a board's name, which adds an Open board link.
  7. Save. It goes through SlickFast to your Slack and email by itself.

Change an alert, or mute it for a while

  • Change it (threshold, pending period, name): edit its rule in your Grafana, Alerting → Alert rules.
  • Mute it for a while: the Silence link in its message, then pick how long.

Ready-made alert rules

Setup page → box 5 → tick only what you run. Each pack says what it watches and what it needs:

  • Servers: server down, CPU busy, memory or disk almost full, disk fills within a day, overloaded. Needs node exporter.
  • Websites: site down, slow, certificate expiring. Needs the blackbox exporter.
  • Postgres: database down, connections nearly full, deadlocks, cache misses. Needs the postgres exporter.
  • Redis: Redis down, evicting keys, refusing connections. Needs the redis exporter.
  • Apps: errors rising, slow responses. Needs your app's request metrics.

Make my alert rules → Download, then your Grafana admin puts the file in Grafana's provisioning/alerting/ folder and restarts Grafana.

Change where alerts go

  • Another Slack channel or inbox: setup page → box 2.
  • Keep your old destination too (PagerDuty, an on-call email): in your Grafana, add SlickFast as an extra integration on the contact point you already use (setup page, box 3, part c). Every alert then goes to both.
  • Only some alerts through SlickFast: a child notification policy that matches a label (e.g. severity = critical), contact point SlickFast, with Continue matching on.
Boards

Boards

Put your Grafana dashboards on a phone or a TV

  • See everything first: setup page → box 4 → Show everything. Every dashboard you have, drawn as a SlickFast board. Press Go live on the ones you want.
  • Or one at a time: box 4 → Load → pick a dashboard → Preview → Go live.

Each board gets its own link and updates by itself; the top line says how often. Nothing changes in your Grafana.

Ready-made boards

Box 4 shows each template with a picture. ✓ Ready means your Grafana already has what it reads; Needs: … says what's missing (e.g. the postgres exporter). Pick a Ready one → Use → Preview → Go live. Want a board of your own design? Email us. Custom boards are part of the service.

Share a board, or put it on a TV

  • Share the whole link, including the ?k=… part. That key is what keeps the board private.
  • Phone / Screen buttons at the top switch shape. For a wall TV, open the link in its browser and press Screen. The page reloads itself, so the TV can be left alone.
  • Tap any tile to see it big, over 1 h, 6 h, 24 h or 7 days.
  • The link works inside your network. Boards that open anywhere (outside your network) use your SlickFast key; see SECURITY.md for exactly what that sends.

Send a board on a schedule

Setup page → box 6 → pick a board (or the weekly report), the time and days, and who gets it, e.g. a client's inbox → Save. Send now to check it.

Rename, change or remove a board

Setup page → box 4 → Your boards: change its title, phone or screen, how often it updates (every 1, 2, 5 or 15 minutes, or every hour), or Remove it (its link stops working).

Everyday

Update, check, remove

Update SlickFast

Download the new files from your Polar page and copy them into the same slickfast folder on your server, the same way as at install. Then, on the server as the Docker user, in that folder:

bash install.sh

Your settings, boards and history are kept, and there's nothing to redo in Grafana.

Check it's working

In your Grafana: Alerting → Contact points → SlickFast → Test. A test card should arrive in your Slack and email within seconds. Nothing? Email hello@slickfast.com.

Remove it completely

SECURITY.md → "Remove it completely": three commands on the server, then delete the slickfast service account and the SlickFast contact point in Grafana.
