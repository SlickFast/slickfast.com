Why now

Self-hosted Grafana lost its alert pictures.

Pictures in alerts needed a separate Chrome-based renderer service, documented at 16 GiB of memory and 4 cores. Grafana 13 (April 2026) removed that renderer plugin, and OnCall OSS was archived in March 2026. Most self-hosted alerts are now a line of text.

The graph, not a link

The card graphs the alert rule's own query for the window around the alert, with the threshold drawn in. You see what happened without logging in.

Less noise

"No Data" and data-source errors are grouped and kept out of your alert channel, so a real alert stands out.

On your server

One container next to Grafana. It reads Grafana with a viewer key and sends through your own email server or your Slack. We never host your data.

What you get

Alerts and live boards.

Every alert and board comes in two shapes: phone (Slack, chat) and screen (email, wall TV). Plus a weekly email of what fired.

How it works

We install it with you.

One call, your screen, your server. In our last test install on a fresh Grafana 12, the first alert arrived 3 min 53 s after we started. Plan on half an hour.

Check

Before the call, a short read-only check of your Grafana tells us what will work and what won't. No surprises on the day.

Install

One command starts the connector. A setup page walks through your Grafana key, email or Slack, and the alert rules.

First alert

We fire a test alert together and you see the card arrive. Then your real alerts start coming in with the graph.

A good fit if

  • You run Grafana yourself (not Grafana Cloud).
  • Your alerts go to email or Slack.

  • You want your data to stay on your servers.
