Why now

Self-hosted Grafana lost its alert pictures.

Pictures in alerts needed a separate Chrome-based renderer service, documented at 16 GiB of memory and 4 cores. Grafana 13 (April 2026) removed that renderer plugin, and OnCall OSS was archived in March 2026. Most self-hosted alerts are now a line of text.

The graph, not a link

The card graphs the alert rule's own query for the window around the alert, with the threshold drawn in. You see what happened without logging in.

Less noise

"No Data" and data-source errors are grouped and kept out of your alert channel, so a real alert stands out.

On your server

One container next to Grafana. It reads Grafana with a viewer key and sends through your own email server or your Slack. We never host your data.

Security

Built to pass your security review.

One page for your IT team comes with every install. The short version:

Read-only

It uses a Grafana key with the Viewer role. It cannot change dashboards, alert rules, users or settings. Revoke the key any time.

No phone home

No usage statistics, no remote access, no automatic updates. SlickFast staff cannot see your data or reach the connector.

Your mail, encrypted

Email goes through your own mail server, encrypted. It refuses to send a password over an unencrypted connection.

Check what you run

Every release ships with checksums the installer verifies. The installer is plain shell and the code inside is readable.

Small footprint

One container, running as a normal user (not root), no special Docker permissions. One port, which you keep inside your network.

Easy to remove

Three commands remove it completely. Alert history keeps facts only (no graphs, no dashboard data) for 60 days.
