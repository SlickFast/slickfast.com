Privacy Policy

Effective July 13, 2026 · SlickWorks LLC

1. Scope

This policy covers slickfast.com and the SlickFast API. The local MCP tool renders entirely on your machine and sends us nothing — this policy is about the website and the hosted API.

2. What we collect

Your email; a stored API key; usage and metering data (render counts, timestamps); the chart data you send us to render or store as live charts; and basic technical logs (IP and request metadata) for security and abuse prevention. Payment details go directly to Polar — we never see or store your card.

3. How we use it

To provide and meter the service, bill you (via Polar), prevent abuse, and provide support. We do not sell your data.

4. Who we share with

Polar (payments and tax), our hosting and CDN providers (to run the service), and no one else except where legally required.

5. Live-chart data

Live-chart data is not private. Live charts are served at unguessable ("unlisted") URLs. Unguessable is not the same as private — anyone who has the link can view the current chart. Treat the link like the data it shows; sharing the link shares the data. Delete a live chart to revoke its link at any time.

6. Retention

We keep account and key data while your account is active. Live charts expire after 90 days without updates (any data push resets the clock), after which they are deleted and their URLs stop resolving. Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for security.

7. Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your data, and you can delete your live charts yourself at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights. We honor GDPR- and CCPA-style requests regardless of where you are located.

8. Cookies

The site is static and sets no tracking cookies. Checkout happens on Polar's pages under Polar's policies.

9. Changes & contact

We will post updates here with a new effective date. Questions: feedback@slickfast.com
